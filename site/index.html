Use cases

What you can connect

Typical scenarios. These are examples, not customer stories.

Ask questions about your database

Connect PostgreSQL or MySQL in read-only mode. Your assistant can answer questions about orders or stock while columns such as passwords and emails stay hidden.

Search your documents locally

Point the server at a folder of Markdown, text or CSV files. Files stay on your machine and any path outside the folder is refused.

Connect a store or SaaS API

Expose only the endpoints you list, remove sensitive fields from every response and keep the token on your own machine.
