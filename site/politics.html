Terms of Service

2. Description of the service. MCP Builder is a platform that generates MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers through an online payment process. The user configures the parameters of the desired server, makes a one-time payment, and receives a downloadable file with the complete server.

3. Payment model. Each MCP server is sold separately. The price varies by the type of data source selected ($5, $15 or $20 USD per server). There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect. We only collect strictly necessary information: your email address (provided during payment) and the technical details of the MCP server you asked us to generate.

2. Use of information. Your email is used only to send you the purchase receipt and download instructions. We do not send spam or share your email with third parties.

Refund Policy

7-day money-back guarantee

If the generated MCP server does not work as described in the documentation, you are entitled to a full refund within the first 7 days after purchase.

How do I request a refund?

  1. Contact our support team through the contact form.
  2. Include your order ID (#MB-XXXXXX) and a description of the problem.
  3. Our team will review your case and process the refund within 48 hours.

Cases not covered. Refunds are not issued if the problem is caused by incorrect user configuration, missing system requirements (e.g. Node.js not installed), or improper use of the MCP server.
